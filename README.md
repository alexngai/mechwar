## Features

- **Three mechs, 34 parts.** Light, Medium and Heavy, each fitted in the hangar with two weapons, an offensive item, a support item and an ability. There are 8 primaries, 10 secondaries, 5 offensive items, 4 support items and 7 abilities.
- **Seven modes:** Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch, Missile Assault, Siege, Capture the Flag, Convoy, and Co-op Defense against waves of the Bloom.
- **Thirteen maps,** from a 120 m waste plant to a 360 m forest, built in layers: undercities, viaducts, mine benches, a city on both sides of a chasm.
- **Movement that matters.** Boosting and hovering burn fuel, sideways dashes dodge fire, and guns heat up and lock if you push them. Hold R to shut down and repair.
- **Bots fill every empty seat,** in every mode. Ping an enemy, or a spot to send them to.
- **Cockpit or chase camera,** with lock-on missiles, deployable shield domes, turrets and mines.
- **Levels and unlocks.** Matches earn XP, up to level 99, and each level unlocks more weapons and items for the mech you choose.
- **Touch controls** on phones and tablets: a stick where your thumb lands, drag to look, aim assist, a TARGET lock-on you can flick out of, and optional gyro aim.
- **Practice offline** on any map with every weapon unlocked.

### Play on a phone

<img src="docs/phone.webp" alt="MECHWAR on a phone held sideways, with the touch controls">

Hold the phone sideways. The stick appears wherever your left thumb lands, and pushing it forward past its rim boosts. Drag anywhere else to look, including while holding FIRE. TARGET locks on to the enemy nearest your sight, and a quick flick breaks the lock. Aim assist, look speed and gyro aim are in the settings.

## Feedback and suggestions

Found a bug, or have an idea for a weapon, a map or a mode? Open an issue. There are forms for both:

- [**Report a bug**](https://github.com/alexngai/mechwar/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml): what happened, what you expected, and your browser and device.
- [**Suggest something**](https://github.com/alexngai/mechwar/issues/new?template=suggestion.yml): a weapon, a map, a mode, a balance change, anything that would make it more fun.

Look through the [open issues](https://github.com/alexngai/mechwar/issues) first. If yours is already there, a 👍 on it helps decide what comes next.

## Contributing

Pull requests are welcome: fixes, maps, weapons, bot behaviour, touch controls, performance on phones. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) covers running it locally, how the code fits together, and how to send a change.

The quick version, with Rust (and its `wasm32-unknown-unknown` target), [wasm-pack](https://github.com/drager/wasm-pack) and Node 22:

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

## How it works

A deterministic Rust simulation ([Rapier](https://rapier.rs) physics) runs the match on the server at 30 Hz. The same Rust code is compiled to WebAssembly, so the browser predicts your own mech and replays the server's corrections; hitscan is lag-compensated. The client draws it with [three.js](https://threejs.org). Every gameplay constant lives in [`tuning.json`](tuning.json).
